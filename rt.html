A Kampala studio built on craft

EF Graphic Designers is a graphic design and printing studio based in Kampala, Uganda. We work on everything from single business card orders to full product branding — business cards, mugs, caps, clothing, posters, flyers and product labels.

Whether you're a business placing a bulk order or an individual customizing a one-off gift, we handle the design and the printing so you get something ready to use.
